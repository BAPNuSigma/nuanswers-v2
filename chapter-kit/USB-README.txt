WHAT THIS IS
An AI teaching assistant for accounting & finance students.
It guides students to answers Socratically (never gives the
answer away), reads their course materials, and shows
professors exactly where the class is struggling.

Built by students, for students — and now for your
chapter too.

WHAT'S ON THIS USB
  1. The complete source code (the nuanswers-v2 folder)
  2. chapter-kit/README.md ...... full setup guide (~1 hour)
  3. chapter-kit/CUSTOMIZE.md ... adapt it to your school

START HERE
Open chapter-kit/README.md in any text editor and follow
the numbered steps. You'll need free GitHub, Vercel, and
Supabase accounts plus an OpenAI key (~$10 to start).

See it live: https://nuanswers.org

Questions? Reach out to the BAP Nu Sigma chapter at FDU.
